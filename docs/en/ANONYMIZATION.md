# Anonymization scope

This public edition uses an independent export without the private working history.

## Measures applied

- Company, project, campaign, commercial portal, and internal source names are replaced with generic labels.
- Original and processed databases, identifiers, and record-level predictions are excluded entirely.
- All original models are excluded. Demonstration artifacts are generated from scratch with synthetic data and a fixed seed.
- Stored notebook outputs, attachments, and execution metadata are removed.
- Original figures, CRM and Power BI screenshots, internal manuals, and PDF appendices are excluded.
- Examples of observed dictionary values are removed; definitions and aggregate percentages are retained.
- Enrichment with prices, addresses, and commercial catalogs is replaced with an explicitly generic adaptation.
- Mappings between generic labels and original entities are excluded.

## Results retained

Aggregate thesis metrics and counts are retained with the author's authorization. Real records are not published under pseudonyms. The demonstration uses independently invented records.

## Differences from the original thesis

The public PDF is an adapted edition, identified on its cover. It omits visual material and operational appendices and does not replace the original academic document. Notebooks retain the research logic with generic references and require private data to reproduce the historical study. They must not be interpreted as a historical training workflow that can run on the synthetic demonstration data.

The English and Spanish portfolio pages follow the same confidentiality boundaries. The academic PDF remains in Spanish.
