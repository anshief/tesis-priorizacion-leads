# The complete dashboard gallery

All eight dashboard screens, adapted for public viewing. Layouts and aggregate charts are retained in a black-and-white edition; company branding and identifying labels are removed or replaced. The prediction table uses fictitious examples.

[Download the public eight-page PDF →](../dashboard/dashboard-public.pdf)

These are static screenshots, not a live Power BI app. Menus and filters pictured in the images are not interactive. Original chart labels remain in Spanish; the explanations below are in English.

## 01 / Navigation

The original navigation layout is retained. The commercial building photograph and company identity are replaced with a neutral Montserrat cover.

[![Navigation — public anonymized edition](../assets/dashboard-01.png)](../assets/dashboard-01.png)

## 02 / Lead performance

Aggregate conversion by channel, campaign, and period. Campaign and portal names are generic labels local to each view, with no published mapping to real entities.

[![Lead performance — public anonymized edition](../assets/dashboard-02.png)](../assets/dashboard-02.png)

## 03 / Sales funnel

Overall conversion and progression between successive stages describe different denominators. K denotes rounded thousands. Loss reasons are aggregate categories, not customer notes.

[![Sales funnel — public anonymized edition](../assets/dashboard-03.png)](../assets/dashboard-03.png)

## 04 / Campaign outliers

Campaign volume and conversion reveal outliers. Identifying labels, including a broker name, have been replaced; bubble positions and sizes are preserved.

[![Campaign outliers — public anonymized edition](../assets/dashboard-04.png)](../assets/dashboard-04.png)

## 05 / Campaigns and visit types

Campaign and visit categories connect acquisition activity with conversion. Identifying company and portal labels are generalized.

[![Campaigns and visit types — public anonymized edition](../assets/dashboard-05.png)](../assets/dashboard-05.png)

## 06 / Demographics

Complete-record subsets only. Field availability varies, so charts can have different denominators; counts must not be added together or treated as the full CRM. Associations do not establish causation.

[![Demographics — public anonymized edition](../assets/dashboard-06.png)](../assets/dashboard-06.png)

## 07 / Financial profile and conversion

Aggregate income bands, desired installments, and purchase purpose. The four commercial price cards are redacted. These historical associations do not determine individual eligibility or future outcomes.

[![Financial profile and conversion — public anonymized edition](../assets/dashboard-07.png)](../assets/dashboard-07.png)

## 08 / Prediction interface

The entire record-level table is replaced with six invented examples, explicitly marked SYNTHETIC / DATOS FICTICIOS. IDs, percentiles, profiles, channels, and stages in those rows are illustrative and are not model outputs. Surrounding aggregate panels retain the source snapshot. Cluster labels and rates reflect that dashboard iteration and should not be equated with the final thesis segmentation; use the thesis for final reported results.

[![Prediction interface — public anonymized edition](../assets/dashboard-08.png)](../assets/dashboard-08.png)

## Publication boundaries

Original attachments, real customer rows and predictions, the company logo, business names, commercial price values, the building photograph, embedded resources, and source notes are not distributed. PNGs and the flattened PDF contain only the reviewed public views; the PDF does not retain the original text or objects underneath redactions.

This gallery documents a historical analytical interface, not measured production impact. [Explore the case study](CASE_STUDY.md) or [run the independent synthetic scoring demo](REPRODUCIBILITY.md).
