# 05 / Test the sales effort

**Does prioritization concentrate historical conversions better than random selection?**

## What this phase delivers

In the reported evaluation population, the top 5% captures 223 of 739 conversions. Estimated effort is about 5 contacts per conversion, versus 31 under random selection.

## Why it leads forward

The historical evaluation supports an interface for prioritization; prospective production impact still requires a controlled trial.

**Historical, non-causal evidence:** this is not a sales increase measured in production.
